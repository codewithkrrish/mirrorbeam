mirrorbeam

rotate the mirrors. guide the laser. light every target.

Solved

Click a mirror to rotate it 90°. The laser fires from the emitter and bounces off mirrors — / and \ bend it different ways. Light every amber target to clear the level. Grey blocks swallow the beam. Fixed mirrors (with a dot) can't be turned, so plan around them.
Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space rotates, R resets, H hints, N next level. Par is the fewest moves any solution needs — the game works it out itself with a built-in solver.
